ORCA ALBUM ARTWORK
CLIENT WORK
The Orca album artwork bridges the gap between raw, tactile expression and precise digital execution. The objective was to craft a visual identity that mirrors the sonic depth of the record—chaotic yet deeply intentional, serving as the visual anchor across both physical vinyl and digital streaming platforms.
DESCRIPTION:
Modified bespoke physical painting scanned and digitally manipulated.

TYPEFACES:
‘Kern’ by Pizza Typefaces.
(A) ‘Tenebras’ by Doménico Barreto.

Vinyl layout by Northary Studio.
COVER ARTWORK

ART DIRECTION

DIGITAL ART
The foundation of the cover began entirely offline. To escape the pristine, sterile nature of a purely digital workflow, the base texture was built through a bespoke physical painting. By manipulating physical mediums and utilizing aggressive brushwork, I established a gritty, visceral backdrop. This piece was then scanned at an ultra-high resolution to capture every imperfection, shadow, and ridge.
Once digitized, the scan was heavily manipulated to push the contrast and carve out the final atmospheric composition. The typography was selected to contrast this chaotic background with razor-sharp authority. Pairing the brutalist 'Kern' with the stylized tension of 'Tenebras' created an aggressive, editorial layout. These final assets were then seamlessly adapted for the physical vinyl sleeve by Northary Studio.
